# Chortle domain glossary

## Puzzle

A daily word-and-chess challenge with a canonical solution line. A player
completes a guess by matching both the word feedback and the required chess
moves.

## Replay session

The inspectable, navigable view of a puzzle's canonical solution line, including
the current position, optional alternate moves, and the analysis that explains
the position.

## Canonical solution line

The ordered chess moves published as the puzzle's intended continuation.

## Alternate move

A legal move played while inspecting a replay that is not the next move on the
canonical solution line.
