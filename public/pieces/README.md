# Chortle piece sets

The additional SVG sets in this directory were sourced from [deverac/piece-packager](https://github.com/deverac/piece-packager), whose collection credits the original artists and sources. They are included here for the development piece-set picker.

- `berlin` — Eric Bentzen; free for personal, non-commercial use; based on the East German Sportverlag design; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `leipzig` — Armando Hernandez Marroquin; freeware; based on chess illustrations from an early German book; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `alpha` — Eric Bentzen; free for personal, non-commercial use; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `merida` — Armando Hernandez Marroquin; GPLv2+; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `maestro` — sadsnake1; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `fantasy` — Maurizio Monge; attribution requested; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `caliente` — Leonid Gordenin (“avi”); CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `horsey` — cham and michael1241; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `pixel` — therealqtpi; AGPLv3+; source: <https://sharechess.github.io>
- `mono` — Thibault Duplessis and Colin M. L. Burnett; source: <https://github.com/deverac/piece-packager>

The `chessnut` files are the existing local set, while Cburnett is bundled by Chessground.
